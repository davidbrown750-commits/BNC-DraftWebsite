SMS / Text Messaging Terms & Conditions

Last updated: July 28, 2026

These SMS Terms & Conditions ("SMS Terms") govern the text messaging program (the "Program") operated by Berkeley Nucleonics Corporation ("Berkeley Nucleonics," "we," "us," or "our"). By opting in to the Program, you agree to these SMS Terms and to our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

Program summary

Berkeley Nucleonics sends recurring automated text messages to customers who have opted in through our website. Messages include the access/registration codes you requested for free BNC Academy courses, along with related account and service updates. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help.

1. Program description

The Program is a customer care and account-notification messaging service. Once you opt in, we use it to deliver:

  • Access and registration codes you requested for free BNC Academy training courses (for example, TM-1 "The Fundamentals of Test & Measurement Instrumentation");
  • Account, order, shipment, and service updates related to your relationship with Berkeley Nucleonics.

The Program does not send marketing or promotional offers to numbers that have not specifically requested them.

2. How to opt in (consent)

You provide express written consent to receive text messages only by checking the optional SMS consent box on a Berkeley Nucleonics website registration form. The box is unchecked by default and is not required to complete any purchase, registration, or request. The checkbox reads:

"By checking this box, I agree to receive recurring automated text messages from Berkeley Nucleonics. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help," with links to our Privacy Policy and these SMS Terms.

Consent is collected only through this optional checkbox. We do not add numbers to the Program from any purchased, rented, or third-party list.

3. Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your activity and requests (for example, when you request a course access code or when there is an update to your account or order). Because these are transactional and account-related messages, you will generally receive them only in response to an action you have taken.

4. Cost / message & data rates

Message and data rates may apply. Berkeley Nucleonics does not charge for the messages themselves, but your mobile carrier's standard message and data rates may apply to each message sent or received. Please contact your wireless provider for details about your plan.

5. Opt out — STOP

You can cancel the Program at any time by texting STOP to the number from which you received our messages. After you send STOP, we will send you a one-time confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, and you will no longer receive messages from that Program. To rejoin, opt in again through our website form.

6. Help — HELP

For help at any time, reply HELP to any message, or contact us at info@berkeleynucleonics.com or (800) 234-7858 / (415) 453-9955. Replying HELP returns Program information and a customer-care contact.

7. Supported carriers & delivery

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Message delivery is subject to effective transmission by your wireless carrier and is not guaranteed.

8. Example messages

Berkeley Nucleonics: Your code for free registration to TM-1 "The Fundamentals of Test & Measurement Instrumentation" is STUDENT_BNC_26. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.
Berkeley Nucleonics: Update to your BNC Academy account. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

9. Privacy & your mobile information

Your privacy is important to us. Please review our full Privacy Policy. With respect to the Program specifically:

No mobile information — including your phone number, SMS opt-in, or consent — will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third parties for their own marketing. Information is shared only with service providers (such as our messaging platform) strictly as needed to operate the Program, and those providers are contractually restricted from using it for any other purpose.

10. Changes to these SMS Terms

We may update these SMS Terms from time to time. Changes are effective when posted to this page, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.
