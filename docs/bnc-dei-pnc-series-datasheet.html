PNC-Series High-Voltage DC Power Supplies

Precisely regulated DC high voltage from 600 V to 300,000 V, with the lowest residual ripple and best long-term stability. The PNC family runs as a benchtop instrument or a 19-inch rack-mount, is continuously short-circuit and reverse-voltage proof, and reaches up to 2,000 W standard. Three variants share one design: standard PNC, high-precision PNChp at 10 ppm ripple, and three-phase PNC3p to 60,000 V and 6,000 W.

1Overview

The PNC series is a family of switched-mode DC high-voltage power supplies from the Berkeley Nucleonics Directed Energy Division. Each unit delivers a precisely regulated DC output with very low residual ripple and excellent long-term stability, and is available in a wide range of voltage classes from 600 V up to 300,000 V. Industrial customers and research institutes worldwide rely on the series where reliability, functional safety, and stability over long runs are the deciding factors.

A universal housing lets the same unit serve as a benchtop instrument or as a 19-inch rack-mount, so the supply fits a lab bench and a production rack equally well. A broad option list, from rapid discharge and ARC detection to polarity reversal and digital interfaces, lets complex high-voltage configurations come together quickly. Modifications to meet individual customer requirements can be made at any time.

The series spans three variants on a common platform. Standard PNC covers power ratings up to 2 kW with accuracy to better than 0.01 percent. PNChp raises precision to a residual ripple of 10 ppm and stability to better than 0.001 percent for the most demanding measurement work. PNC3p adds a three-phase input for higher power, up to 60,000 V and 6,000 W.

Looking for programmable benchtop 1.5 kV to 30 kV DC supplies? See the HVX-Series high-voltage power supplies.

6Control & Interfaces

Output values are set locally through separate 10-turn potentiometers for voltage and current, with a 3.5-digit digital display for each. HV on and off run from a front-panel push button or from the interface. CV and CC mode each have a status LED, and an HV-ON signal lamp confirms the live state.

An integrated analog interface makes the supply remotely controllable and extendable. Voltage and current are each adjustable over 0 to 10 V, with separate 0 to 10 V monitor outputs for voltage and current. Output on and off uses a normally-open contact, where a closed contact means on. The interface terminates on a 15-pin Sub-D socket.

Selected Options

The PNC platform carries a broad option list. Frequently specified options include all outputs on the rear side (Option 01, standard above 35 kV), interlock connection (Option 02), 4.5-digit displays (Option 04), DC isolation of the analog interface (Option 10), coarse/fine setup control (Option 22), power control (Option 41), ramp control (Option 46), rapid discharge circuit (Option 52), ARC detection with power cut (Option 56), voltage and current limit setting (Option 57), output polarity reversal (Option 60 / Option 61), and calibration certificate (Option 95). Available options vary by variant.

7Protection & Safety

  • Continuous short-circuit proof. The output withstands a sustained short circuit without damage.
  • Reverse-voltage proof. The supply tolerates reverse voltage at the output.
  • Controlled discharge. Internal discharge brings the output down in less than 60 s without load (type-dependent); a rapid discharge circuit is available (Option 52).
  • ARC detection. Optional ARC detection with power cut (Option 56) protects sensitive loads from flashover events.
  • Earth-referenced output. The HV output socket is connected to earth and passed through to the output voltage.
  • Interlock. An interlock connection is available (Option 02) for integration with system safety circuits.

8Applications

The PNC family serves precision DC high-voltage needs across industry and research, wherever reliability and long-term stability matter.

  • Semiconductor test and manufacturing
  • High-voltage test and quality test
  • Mass spectrometer detector biasing
  • Accelerators, including kickers and detectors
  • Plasma applications (PNC3p)
  • Electron-gun applications (PNC3p)
  • Precision metrology and reference work (PNChp)

For capacitive accelerator and beam-deflection loads that need fast pulsed high voltage rather than steady DC, the Directed Energy Division also offers a range of high-voltage pulse generators. Contact Berkeley Nucleonics to match the supply or pulser to your load.

9Ordering Information

Specify the variant (PNC, PNChp, or PNC3p), the voltage class and current rating from the model grid, the polarity, and any options. Each device is available with positive or negative polarity (positive x = 1 or negative x = 9 in the part number). Standard scope of supply for PNC and PNChp includes the BNC PNC unit per type description, a 3 m BNC HV cable with HV connector, a 1.5 m power cable with connector (CEE7), and a plug for the analog interface. The three-phase PNC3p ships with the unit per type description, a 3 m HV cable with HV connector, a plug for the analog interface, and a user manual (German / English).
