Under $10K: The 20 GHz Benchtop Arrives

Just launched — the first 20 GHz benchtop RF signal generator under $10,000.

Calibrated to 20 GHz and settable to 21.5 GHz, with sub-2 Hz frequency resolution and up to +15 dBm output — plus an integrated touchscreen, front-panel control, and LAN remote for automated test. At its price it finally opens the door for teaching labs, start-up R&D, and wireless/aerospace benches that could never justify a $30–40K box. Get it in front of every RF and T&M account you have.

Spectroscopic ID, Personal Size

SAM 920+ SPRD — now shipping.

RIID-class isotope ID in a pocket-sized personal detector: a CLLBC scintillator for simultaneous gamma and neutron, sourceless auto-stabilization (no check source), plus GPS, camera, and one-touch reach-back. Built to deploy at scale for security, first responders, and safeguards.
