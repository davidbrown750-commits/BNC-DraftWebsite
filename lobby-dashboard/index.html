The lobby sign

The original reception board: month-to-date sales, the Cal Lab queue, yesterday's email figures, news, the weather and the engineer's riddle. Reception can switch back to it at any time. Notices, riddles, photos and shows on it are edited here.

Screens

Screens are shown on the reception TV at /screen/?id=<name>. That page asks for the screen password once per device, then remembers it for a year.

Lobby sign blocks

Each block is one piece of content on the sign. Notices ride the amber slot between the news items, riddles join the hourly rotation, photos take over the replies panel, and shows appear in Upcoming. The sign picks changes up within a minute.
